   - Level 3 Head: 1120 ("Trade Accounts (Parties)")
   - Har party ka apna Level 4 account auto-create aur auto-sync hota hai (e.g. 11200001, 11200002).
   - Kisi bhi party se advance lia ja sakta hai, kisi ko bhi advance diya ja sakta hai,
     kisi se bhi purchase aur kisi ko bhi sell ki ja sakti hai.
d. Har party ka mukammal Party Ledger banta hai jisme purchase bills, sales invoices,
   aur vouchers (CPV, CRV, BPV, BRV, JV) running balance ke sath aate hain.
e. Cash Ledger aur Daily Cash Report voucher entry se direct connect ho chuki hain.
   Jo bhi paisa cash in hand me aya ya gaya wo cash ledger aur cash report me reflect hota hai.
f. Proper User Management System:
   - Form sab se upar (Top) mojood hai: Username, Password, Role (Admin/Staff), Mobile, Email, Address.
   - Form ke theek neechay registered users ka responsive table mojood hai.

3. VOUCHER TYPES & ACCOUNTING MECHANICS (CPV, CRV, BPV, BRV, JV)
================================================================================
System me double-entry accounting rules ke mutabiq 5 tabs hain:

1. CPV (Cash Payment Voucher):
   - Prefix: CPV-YYYY-XXXXX (e.g. CPV-2026-00001)
   - Kab use karna hai: Jab aap Cash in Hand se kisi party ko advance dein,
     kisi supplier ko purchase payment karein, ya company ka expense pay karein.
   - Accounting Rule:
     * Credit: Cash in Hand [1110001] (Raqam kam hogi)
     * Debit:  Party Account [1120000X] ya Expense Account [521000X] (Raqam barhegi/bill pay hoga)
   - Effect: CashRecord me Cash Out hoga aur Cash in Hand balance decrease hoga.
             Party Ledger me payment show hogi aur party ka balance adjust hoga.

2. CRV (Cash Receipt Voucher):
   - Prefix: CRV-YYYY-XXXXX (e.g. CRV-2026-00001)
   - Kab use karna hai: Jab aap Cash in Hand me kisi party se advance lein,
     sales invoice ki cash collection karein, ya koi aamdani receive karein.
   - Accounting Rule:
     * Debit:  Cash in Hand [1110001] (Cash barhega)
     * Credit: Party Account [1120000X] ya Income Account (Party due kam hogi ya advance barhega)
   - Effect: CashRecord me Cash In hoga aur Cash in Hand balance increase hoga.
             Party Ledger me Credit me aayega aur customer ki due kam hogi.

3. BPV (Bank Payment Voucher):
   - Prefix: BPV-YYYY-XXXXX (e.g. BPV-2026-00001)
   - Kab use karna hai: Jab aap kisi Bank Account (e.g. Bank Al-Habib, Meezan, MCB)
     se cheque ya online transfer ke zariye payment ya advance karein.
   - Accounting Rule:
     * Credit: Selected Bank Account [1110002 / 1110003 / 1110004] (Bank balance kam hoga)
     * Debit:  Party Account ya Expense Account
   - Effect: CashRecord me cash_in_bank decrease hoga.

4. BRV (Bank Receipt Voucher):
   - Prefix: BRV-YYYY-XXXXX (e.g. BRV-2026-00001)
   - Kab use karna hai: Jab party aapke bank account me online paisay bhejay
     ya cheque deposit ho.
   - Accounting Rule:
     * Debit:  Selected Bank Account [1110002 / 1110003 / 1110004] (Bank balance barhega)
     * Credit: Party Account ya Income Account
   - Effect: CashRecord me cash_in_bank increase hoga.

5. JV (Journal / Transfer Voucher & Multi-line Grid):
   - Prefix: JV-YYYY-XXXXX (e.g. JV-2026-00001)
   - Kab use karna hai: Jab Cash se Bank me paisay jama karwayen (Contra),
     Bank se Cash nikala jaye, ya Party-to-Party adjustment karni ho.
   - Multi-line Grid mode me multiple Debit aur Credit rows add ki ja sakti hain
     jahan Total Debit = Total Credit hona zaroori hai.

4. CHART OF ACCOUNTS (COA) LEVEL 4 & PARTY INTEGRATION
================================================================================
Chart of Accounts ka 4-Level structure:
- Level 1: Main Head (1000 Assets, 2000 Liabilities, 3000 Equity, 4000 Revenue, 5000 Expenses)
- Level 2: Sub Head (1100 Current Assets, 5200 Operating Expenses, etc.)
- Level 3: Control Head:
  * 1110: Cash & Bank Balances
  * 1120: Trade Accounts (Parties)  <--- YAHAN HAR PARTY EXIST KARTI HAI!
  * 5210: Daily Operating Expenses
- Level 4: Transactional Accounts:
  * 1110001: Cash in Hand
  * 1110002: Bank Al-Habib
  * 1110003: Meezan Bank
  * 1110004: MCB Bank
  * 11200001: Party 1 (Linked to Parties model via party_id)
  * 11200002: Party 2
  * ... tamam parties Level 4 par mojood hain.

Party Sync Mechanism:
- Jab bhi aap Parties page se nayi party banate hain ya edit karte hain,
  system automatically `sync_single_party_to_coa()` call karta hai.
- Agar party pehle se na ho to uska Level 4 code generate hota hai aur
  Parties table ke sath foreign key se link ho jata hai.
- Tamam 11 parties Level 4 par sync ho chuki hain.

5. PARTY LEDGER, CASH LEDGER & REPORTS INTEGRATION
================================================================================
1. Party Ledger (/party_ledger/):
   - Kisi bhi party ka ledger select karein aur date filter lagayein.
   - Opening Balance: Date range se pehle ke tamam Purchase bills, Sales invoices,
     aur Vouchers (CPV, CRV, BPV, BRV, JV) ka net opening balance calculate hota hai.
   - Period Transactions:
     * Purchase Bill: Credit (Hum ne party ko dena hai)
     * Sales Invoice: Debit (Party ne humein dena hai)
     * CPV / BPV (Payment/Advance to party): Debit (Hum ne de diya)
     * CRV / BRV (Receipt/Advance from party): Credit (Party ne de diya)
   - Har line par running balance calculate hota hai.
   - PDF Export button se mukammal statement print/download ho sakti hai.

2. Cash Ledger (/cash_ledger/):
   - Har roz cash in hand aur bank me aane wale aur jane wale paison ka
     complete debit/credit ledger banta hai.
   - CPV aur BPV cash out / payments me aate hain.
   - CRV aur BRV cash in / receipts me aate hain.

3. Daily Cash Report (/cashreport/):
   - Left Side: Receipts / Sales & CRV/BRV vouchers + Previous Day Closing.
   - Right Side: Purchase Payments, CPV/BPV vouchers & Expenses.
   - Bottom: Net Cash in Hand & Cash in Bank closing balances.

4. Purchase Reports (/purchase_report/):
   - Purchase receiving se generate hoti hai, party-wise aur date-wise bills.

5. Sales Reports (/sells_report/):
   - Sales invoices se generate hoti hai.

6. Net Position Report (/net_position/):
   - Tamam parties ki current net position (Receivable / Payable / Advance)
     live calculate karke display karti hai.

8. DATABASE SCHEMA & ORM MODELS SUMMARY
================================================================================
1. `core_chartofaccount`:
   - id (PK)
   - code (VARCHAR 50, Unique): e.g. '1110001', '11200001'
   - name (VARCHAR 200): Head title
   - level (INT): 1, 2, 3, ya 4
   - parent_id (FK to self): Tree hierarchy
   - account_type (VARCHAR 50): Asset, Liability, Equity, Revenue, Expense
   - party_id (FK to core_parties, Nullable): Connects Level 4 to Party!
   - opening_balance, current_balance (DECIMAL 18,2)
   - is_active (BOOLEAN)

2. `core_voucher`:
   - id (PK)
   - voucher_no (VARCHAR 50, Unique): CPV-YYYY-XXXXX, CRV-YYYY-XXXXX, etc.
   - voucher_type (VARCHAR 30): CPV, CRV, BPV, BRV, JV
   - date (DATE)
   - reference_no (VARCHAR 100)
   - total_amount (DECIMAL 18,2)
   - remarks (LONGTEXT)
   - created_by, created_at, updated_at

3. `core_voucherdetail`:
   - id (PK)
   - voucher_id (FK to core_voucher)
   - account_id (FK to core_chartofaccount)
   - party_id (FK to core_parties, Nullable)
   - debit (DECIMAL 18,2)
   - credit (DECIMAL 18,2)
   - narration (VARCHAR 255)

4. `core_parties`:
   - id (PK), code, company_name, owner_name, number, party_type, address

5. `core_users`, `core_userpage`, `core_usermodule`:
   - Proper user authentication and granular page permission control.

6. `cash_records`:
   - Synced with vouchers for cash in hand & cash in bank live tracking.
